    Try an already-sorted input like 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8: Bubble and Insertion Sort finish in one pass (O(n)), Selection Sort still makes every comparison, and Quick Sort with a last-element pivot hits its O(n²) worst case. Counting and Radix Sort show 0 comparisons: they place values by counting instead of comparing, which is how they beat the O(n log n) limit that applies to comparison sorts.
